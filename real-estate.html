Real Estate

  • Home
  • Practice Areas
  • Real Estate

At Dhwaj & Associates, our Real Estate Law practice is dedicated to providing comprehensive legal support tailored to the diverse needs of our clients in the real estate sector. Our experienced team specializes in advising clients on all aspects of real estate transactions, ensuring that every detail is meticulously handled from inception to completion. Whether you are involved in the purchase, leasing, sale, or mortgage of commercial, industrial, agricultural, or residential properties, we are here to guide you through the process with expertise and confidence.

Our team is well-versed in the intricacies of commercial projects, including hotels, malls, and multiplexes. We assist clients in navigating the complexities of commercial real estate transactions, from drafting and negotiating contracts to ensuring compliance with local regulations. Our comprehensive understanding of market dynamics allows us to provide strategic advice that aligns with your business objectives.

For residential projects, we offer legal support that covers everything from land acquisition to the sale of residential units. Our team ensures that all legal documentation is meticulously prepared, addressing any regulatory requirements and safeguarding your interests throughout the process.

We also specialize in the retail sector, providing legal advice for supermarkets, showrooms, and shops. Our expertise in retail real estate transactions enables us to assist clients in securing favourable leasing agreements and navigating the complexities of local laws and regulations.

When disputes arise, our firm is well-equipped to handle labour disputes and employment claims. We represent clients in negotiations and litigations, advocating for your rights while working towards equitable resolutions. Our experience in this area ensures that you are well-prepared to address any challenges that may arise.

In addition to transaction support, our firm is equipped to handle real estate-related dispute resolution. We represent clients in landlord and tenant disputes, advocating for their rights and working toward equitable solutions. Our approach focuses on effective negotiation and, when necessary, litigation, ensuring that your interests are protected.

Our team is experienced in drafting and reviewing building, development, and construction contracts. We understand the importance of clear and enforceable agreements in real estate projects and work diligently to ensure that all contractual obligations are met while mitigating potential risks.

We also provide guidance on land use regulations, helping clients navigate the complexities of zoning laws and local ordinances. Our comprehensive understanding of these regulations allows us to offer strategic advice that aligns with your development goals.

Furthermore, we assist clients with mortgage and finance agreements, ensuring that financing arrangements are structured to meet your needs while adhering to legal requirements. Our team is committed to providing clear, practical advice that supports your financial objectives.

Our Approach

At Dhwaj & Associates, we are dedicated to all aspects of real estate law. Our commitment to excellence and attention to detail ensures that your transactions are conducted smoothly and efficiently. For more information on how we can assist you with your legal needs, please don't hesitate to contact us at info@dalaw.in

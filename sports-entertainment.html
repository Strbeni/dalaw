Sports & Entertainment Laws

  • Home
  • Practice Areas
  • Sports & Entertainment Laws

At Dhwaj & Associates, our Sports & Entertainment Law practice is distinguished by its deep understanding of the legal, commercial, and regulatory issues that shape this dynamic industry. Our market-leading team of specialists is fully immersed in the business, keeping abreast of the latest developments and opportunities to provide our clients with the most relevant and effective legal counsel. We pride ourselves on delivering straightforward, actionable advice that aligns with our clients' goals, ensuring timely results within budget. Catering to a wide array of clients—including national and international governing bodies, leading clubs, kit manufacturers, sports marketing agencies, player agencies, sponsors, banks, investors, individual athletes, artists, brands, and event organizers—our extensive reach enables us to offer practical and commercial legal advice that meets the unique needs of each client.

Our team is well-equipped to assist with management and agency contracts, ensuring that the interests of both the talent and the agencies are effectively protected. We guide clients through the complexities of sponsorship and merchandising agreements, helping them maximize opportunities while safeguarding their brand and reputation.

In the realm of performing arts, we provide expert advice on performer, producer, and composer rights, including the negotiation and drafting of agreements and releases that clarify rights and obligations. Our experience in live performances and touring ensures that artists and producers can navigate the myriad of legal considerations that arise in these settings.

Additionally, we support clients in establishing clear band structures and partnership agreements, facilitating harmonious relationships and clear expectations among members. Our team also handles the intricacies of distribution, broadcast, syndication rights, and deals, ensuring that our clients’ creative works are effectively marketed and monetized.

As the landscape of entertainment continues to evolve, we are prepared to address the complexities of production and co-production contracts. Our firm guides clients through all phases of production, ensuring compliance with legal standards while fostering successful collaborations.

We also provide essential guidance on all issues arising from key sponsorship agreements and supplier contracts, including shirt sponsorship arrangements and broadcast agreements. Our focus on clarity and enforceability in these agreements helps clients maximize their commercial potential.

For transfer transactions, our team is skilled in navigating the regulatory landscape, and ensuring compliance with applicable laws while facilitating smooth and efficient transfers.

Our Approach

At Dhwaj & Associates, our commitment to excellence and client success in the sports and entertainment sector is unwavering. We are dedicated to helping you achieve your objectives with confidence, providing the legal expertise and strategic insight needed to thrive in this competitive industry.

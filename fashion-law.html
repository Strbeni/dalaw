Fashion Law

  • Home
  • Practice Areas
  • Fashion Law

Fashion law is an innovative practice area that intersects the realms of creativity and legal compliance. At Dhwaj and Associates, we recognize the intricate challenges that designers, brands, and other stakeholders face in the dynamic fashion industry. Our specialized legal expertise focuses on resolving broad spectrum of issues including without limitation issues related to copyright, trademark, patent, trade dress, and design law.

We provide comprehensive legal solutions tailored to the challenges of the fashion industry. We are dedicated to safeguarding creative innovations, helping domestic and international designers navigate the complexities of intellectual property protection and combat the pervasive threat of fashion piracy. Our commitment extends to fashion startups, as we equip them with the essential tools and strategies to enhance their brand value and secure their intellectual property from the outset.

Our services span the entire lifecycle of a luxury brand, ensuring that our clients receive multi-disciplinary legal support that meets their specific needs. From the inception of an idea to market expansion, we offer expert guidance in forming and structuring companies, strategic partnerships, and M&A transactions.

Our team at Dhwaj and Associates is equipped to deliver an extensive array of key services that cater to every aspect of the fashion industry. We prioritize the protection of ideas and brands, ensuring that our clients’ creative concepts are safeguarded from the moment they are conceived. Additionally, we assist in drafting and negotiating agreements with models, influencers, and other stakeholders, fostering successful collaborations that enhance brand visibility. Our expertise also extends to monetization strategies, helping clients unlock the value of their intellectual property through licensing and franchising opportunities.

We are committed to implementing robust anti-counterfeiting measures, protecting our clients from the damaging effects of counterfeit products. In cases of disputes, our litigation services cover the enforcement of intellectual property rights—trademarks, copyrights, designs, patents, and trade secrets. We also guide clients on compliance with ethical and environmental standards, addressing issues such as product labelling and deceptive advertising. As the fashion industry evolves, we are ready to tackle emerging challenges in product liability, consumer protection, and data privacy, ensuring our clients remain compliant and secure in an increasingly complex marketplace.

Taxation

  • Home

  • Taxation

At Dhwaj & Associates, our Tax Team is renowned for providing holistic tax solutions tailored to the unique challenges faced by businesses operating in and with India. We offer a comprehensive suite of services encompassing advisory, litigation, and compliance support across various areas of taxation. Our dedicated team of domain experts handles every aspect of taxation, ensuring that our clients receive specialized assistance whether they are dealing with direct or indirect tax matters.

In the realm of direct taxation, we provide strategic advice on corporate income tax, transfer pricing, and other relevant areas. Our focus is on tax planning and compliance, helping businesses navigate the complexities of the tax landscape while optimizing their tax positions. We also represent our clients before authorities at higher levels, advocating for their interests and ensuring compliance with statutory obligations.

For indirect taxation, our team is well-versed in the intricacies of Goods and Service Tax (GST), customs duties, and service tax regulations. We assist clients in understanding their obligations under these laws and provide guidance on compliance requirements, ensuring that businesses operate within the legal framework while minimizing tax liabilities.

Our comprehensive auditing services include statutory audits, management audits, internal and concurrent audits, and special audits under Section 142(2A) of the Income Tax Act. Through these services, we help clients identify potential issues, enhance operational efficiency, and ensure compliance with regulatory requirements.

In addition to auditing, our taxation services encompass a wide range of functions, including planning, compliance, and representation. We provide expert guidance on corporate taxation matters, ensuring that our clients understand their rights and responsibilities while effectively managing their tax affairs.

Our firm also offers valuable financial services, including business and share valuations, tax due diligence, and due diligence. These services are crucial for clients looking to make informed business decisions, whether they are engaging in mergers and acquisitions, raising capital, or assessing potential investments.

Furthermore, we provide advisory and liaisoning services that include a thorough review of systems and procedures, as well as forensic auditing. This holistic approach ensures that clients have robust internal controls and processes in place to mitigate risks and comply with regulatory requirements.

Our Approach

At Dhwaj & Associates, our commitment to excellence in taxation services sets us apart. We understand the importance of proactive tax management and are dedicated to providing our clients with the insights and support they need to thrive in a complex tax environment.

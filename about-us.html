Our Lawyers Provide Customer Centric Advice to Corporations

In today’s complex and ever-changing legal landscape, having a trusted legal advisor by your side can make all the difference.

Dhwaj & Associates (Firm) is a young, focused, efficient and passionate full-service law firm in India, set up by an experienced and reputed legal practitioner. We have unshakable dedication to our clients' interests, passion for our work and a strong bond with our colleagues. We believe in partnerships and make it a priority to know and understand our clients and their businesses.

We are naturally innovative, commercial and entrepreneurial. Our lawyers’ depth of experience and comprehensive industry focus distinguish us from other law firms and inspire our clients’ and colleagues’ loyalty and satisfaction.

With a single point agenda of maintaining the transparent approach along with in-depth domain knowledge of their respective practice areas, our lawyers are true problem solvers with best fit solutions. Our team has a rare combination of technical excellence and commercial understanding to provide practical legal advice.

The Firm services domestic as well as international clients. Broadly, the practice areas of the Firm include Corporate Commercial (including Mergers and Acquisitions, Foreign Direct Investments, Joint Ventures, Private Equity & Venture Capital, Collaborations and other cross border transactions), Real Estate & Infrastructure Projects, Competition laws, Employment laws, Information Technology law, Media and Entertainment law, Telecommunication law, Taxation laws, Environment laws, Data protection & Privacy, Artificial Intelligence, Dispute Resolution including Arbitration (Domestic and International) and Litigation.

The Firm currently has its office at Noida, Bangalore & Mumbai (India). In addition to being armed with competent lawyers, the Firm has alliances with lawyers/law firms in major cities across India.

The Firm’s strong emphasis towards client’s satisfaction has not only resulted in identifying the client’s need but also anticipating future necessities that entail legal advice primarily on the latest market developments, regulatory changes, legislative actions, commercial and technical issues.

  • Always respect the law of land and encourage others to do so
  • Always act honestly
  • Always protect confidentiality of Clients
  • Always contribute in building a fairer society

At Dhwaj & Associates our mission is to provide a high quality, creative, and result – oriented legal team to individuals and businesses, and walk hand in hand with our clients in all aspects of their growth and development. In pursuit of this Mission, we will hold to these values:

  • For our Clients: We will dedicate ourselves to work hard, provide superior legal services and maintain the highest standards of professional integrity. We will endeavor to meet or exceed the expectations of our clients in all aspects of their legal representation.
  • For our Firm: We will foster an enjoyable working environment, based on open communication and mutual respect, and will encourage initiative, innovation, teamwork, and loyalty.
